01 / Design

Architecture

We shape functional, considered buildings around the brief, site, people, and long-term performance.

Scope of work

  • Site appraisal, project brief review, and design objectives
  • Concept design, options studies, and spatial planning
  • Design development and coordination with engineering consultants
  • Material, sustainability, accessibility, and buildability guidance
  • Design documentation and stakeholder review coordination
  • Support through approvals, tender, and construction-stage design queries

02 / Place

Urban Planning

We align land use, infrastructure, mobility, and public space to create coherent, resilient places.

Scope of work

  • Site context, land-use, and development-potential studies
  • Master plans, precinct plans, and development frameworks
  • Planning policy, constraints, and regulatory review
  • Mobility, access, circulation, and infrastructure strategies
  • Public realm, open space, and community-use planning
  • Phasing strategies and coordination with relevant stakeholders

03 / Delivery

Project Management

We provide governance and coordination that keeps project teams aligned from brief through handover.

Scope of work

  • Project brief, delivery strategy, governance, and responsibilities
  • Programme development, milestone setting, and schedule monitoring
  • Budget tracking and coordination with cost consultants
  • Consultant, contractor, client, and stakeholder coordination
  • Risk, change, quality, and decision management
  • Progress reporting, completion planning, and handover coordination

04 / Value

Cost Consultancy

We bring cost clarity to project decisions, protecting budget viability while supporting design value.

Scope of work

  • Order-of-cost estimates, feasibility advice, and cost benchmarks
  • Elemental cost plans and budget monitoring through design stages
  • Quantity take-offs, bills of quantities, and tender documentation support
  • Tender analysis, commercial comparisons, and recommendation support
  • Value engineering and cost-risk reviews
  • Change control, payment review, and final-account support

05 / Spatial insight

GIS & Remote Sensing

We turn geographic data and earth observation into practical insight for site, planning, and development decisions.

Scope of work

  • GIS mapping, spatial database setup, and data integration
  • Satellite and aerial imagery interpretation
  • Land-use, land-cover, and change-detection analysis
  • Site suitability, proximity, and spatial constraint analysis
  • Terrain, access, utilities, and infrastructure mapping
  • Thematic maps and clear spatial outputs for project teams
